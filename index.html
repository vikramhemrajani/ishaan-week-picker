How to use
Locked blocks (school, social, tutors) stay put. Open blocks start as Free — tap to make them Study IB, Study SAT, or Light. For IB: choose a subject chip, then a topic (or Other / type…). For SAT: choose Reading & Writing, Math, or Full practice, then a topic. Open study/free/light blocks are 45 minutes each (0.75 h). Aim for exactly 12 h IB and exactly 3 h SAT. When ready, tap Send to calendar — that writes General Timetable drafts and updates the shared plan and topic progress every browser can load. Copy plan text is optional for notes.
